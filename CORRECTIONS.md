# Corrections policy

Report an error through <https://gamevgame.com/contact> or `support@gamevgame.com`.

Please include:

1. `relation_id`;
2. the field that is wrong;
3. a first-party or otherwise reliable evidence URL;
4. the proposed correction;
5. the date you checked it.

Accepted corrections are recorded in `CHANGELOG.md`. Identity or factual errors trigger a patch version. Editorial disagreements are reviewed but are not automatically treated as factual errors.
